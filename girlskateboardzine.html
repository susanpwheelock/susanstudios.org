Girl Skateboard Zine

This publication project granted immense creative freedom to research and visually interpret a personally meaningful subculture. I focused on Girl Skateboards, one of the industry's most iconic brands, to deeply explore its cultural impact and history within the skateboarding community.

The visual language relies heavily on a high-contrast palette of blue, black, and white; combining personal preference with strong visual impact. The layout design is structured to mirror the dynamic energy of the sport, creating a flowing composition where the backgrounds evoke a sense of continuous motion.

Scope: Editorial Design, Publication Layout, Brand Research

Software: Adobe InDesign, Adobe Photoshop,
Adobe Illustrator

Year: Spring, 2025
