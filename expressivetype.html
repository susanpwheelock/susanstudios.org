Expressive Type Poster

The objective of this project was to design a typographic composition based on a grid system, exploring the visual representation of the word imperfect. This concept holds deep personal significance, serving as a reminder to balance my perfectionist tendencies with self-compassion and objective self-evaluation.

Visually, the composition uses fragmented letterforms to evoke a broken aesthetic that strives toward structural harmony. I integrated positive affirmations within the negative space between the letters, symbolizing how gaps of imperfection can be filled with encouragement and positivity.

Scope: Typography, Visual Composition, Poster Design

Software: Adobe Illustrator

Year: Fall, 2024
