Global Warming Symbols Booklet

This project features a cohesive iconography system of three symbols representing climate change resistance. The vector icons unfold into an infographic zine that details their underlying meanings and scientific context.

Using a minimal color palette derived from the icons, I created intricate background illustrations throughout the layout. When fully unfolded, the zine reveals a patterned artwork layout; a visual whirlpool of symbols referencing the extreme storms driven by climate shifts.

Scope Iconography System, Information Design, Print Layout

Software: Adobe Illustrator

Year: Spring, 2025
